## 👩‍🔬 About Me

I hold a **PhD in Bioinformatics from the University of Tehran** and currently work as a **Postdoctoral Fellow at the Institute of Biochemistry and Biophysics (IBB), University of Tehran**.

My primary research focus is on **metagenomics** and **enzyme discovery**, particularly the **identification of plastic-degrading enzymes from plastic-polluted metagenomic environments**.

I am interested in applying **bioinformatics, computational biology, and data-driven approaches** to explore microbial diversity, mine functional genes, and discover novel biocatalysts with potential environmental and biotechnological applications.

## 🚀 Current Research Focus

### ♻️ Discovery of Plastic-Degrading Enzymes from Polluted Metagenomic Environments

My PhD and postdoctoral research both focus on the **identification of plastic-degrading enzymes from plastic-polluted metagenomic environments**.

This includes:

- mining metagenomic datasets from plastic-contaminated environments
- identifying enzyme-coding genes with biodegradation potential
- functional annotation of candidate sequences
- prioritizing promising enzymes for downstream analysis
- exploring microbial communities associated with plastic degradation
